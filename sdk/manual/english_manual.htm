1- Presentation 

  What’s your mod ?

It’s an Half Life’s mod, so you have to own Half Life in order to play. 

In our mod, there are three game play : 
  
                The battle royal : Here, the Syndicate selects its members. The survivor of this test is welcomed into the family. 
    
                 In game term, the royal battle is made up of rounds, as soon as a player is killed, he becomes a ghost. When a player spawns, he is given a weapon at random with only one cartridge, but he may be given only one bullet shield and no weapon. All this is managed randomly. The aim is to be alive last.         

                 Frags system : +1 Frags by ennemi killed . +3 Frags for the survivor of the round. +5 Frags for the survivor of the round if he killed himself the last enemy. - 5 for killing himself 
    
                   Max players number : 32   

 

                The Bounty Killer : One hunted and many hunters, everybody wants to kill everybody but the hunted is the best target. The one who killed the hunted become the hunted and can have more frags when he kills the hunters until one hunter takes it place.

                In game terms it is as a classical death match but it's more funny because being the hunted give you more frags when you kill somebody but everybody wants to kill you for becoming the hunted. 
  
                Frags system : +1 Frags for a hunter who killed another hunter. +2 frags by hunters killed if you are the hunted. +10 frags for killing the hunted .- 5 Frags for killing himself 
            
                 Max players number : 32      

 

                The Coop : Brother, fight against our enemies. 

              In game term, at the beginning of the game, the player selects his model, a blade, a light weapon, an heavy weapon, cybertech, and an equipment. Then, the players and his colleagues, other players, spawn and have to fight against the AI (artificial intelligence), the game is made up of rounds, as soon as all the players are dead, the round restart until the level ends. It is probably the most exciting game mode. 
  
             Max players number : 8  

  
2- How to play SBO ?  
 
It’s simple, that’s why there is the present manual:-).In first you have to launch Half Life, but not too far.

Now you have to do CUSTOM GAME 
Then click on « Syndicate Black Ops » 
And just click on ACTIVATE
Instead of the traditional half life’s screen, you should have Syndicate’s screen. Click on Done in order to quit the CUSTOM GAME menu 

3- How to Configure SBO ? 
  
 Once the mod is loaded you have to configure your keyboard, so click on ENTER BROTHER. 

And then CUSTOMIZE.
 
In « player name »…. I let you guess what you have to do :-)

Below you can choose a totally useless thing : your graph 

When it’s done, click DONE :-)


Next ? Have you already forget the keyboard configuration ? 
  
I think not, so go in the CONTROLS section and configure as you like. I’ll be quiet for the HL’s keys, we will directly see the particularity of the mod :   

« Cloak » Lets you toggle between cloaking and normal mode. When you are cloaking, you are partially invisible ( works only with the assassin).    

« Cyber Eye » toggle on or off for the cyber eyes.  

« Change weapon » : use this key in order to change your weapons choice. Changes will be effective at he beginning of the next round    

« Change model » : it’s as the “change weapon” key, but it lets you change your sex.    

« Go To the GM Menu » toggle on/of the game master mode 
  
When you keys are defining, just click OK. 
    
  
4- Playing SBO 
  
Now you are ready to play, but you can quit the game if you fear by clicking DONE and then FEAR? QUIT

Despite clicking on refresh you see nothing ? Deactivate your firewall if you have one, check your connection and you network configuration (for more information read the manual of Windows and/or of your network card). 
   
        - Create a game in order to play alone or in attempt that other players from your LAN join you. In this case do CREATE and go to the next section. 
  
    
5- Create a game 
  
In first you have to choose a map, but be aware that there are three sort of maps : 
   
            Those who beginning by BR_ are Battle Royal maps 
            Those who beginning by BK_ are Bounty Killer maps 
            And those who beginning by CO_ are cooperatives 

  So map choice defines gameplay. 

NB : You can define in advance which maps will be launch once you have finish the first via the mapcycle. 
 
You can enter a "server name", this name will be what players who attempt to connect are seeing.

You have to define the " max players number". 

And you can enter a " password". Only people who know this password will be able to join your game. 
  
Now we are going to see the advanced option, click on ADCANCED 
      There are two pages of advanced options 
   
            In the first you will find : 
  
« Frag Limit » : Enter here the max number of frags before the game is end (this option will not affect cooperative maps because the number of death before the cooperative map is end was entered by the level designer, however you can increase this value by checking Maximum Number of Rounds X 1,5.) 
  
« Time Limit » Enter here the time limit before the game is end (as the frag limit it will not affect cooperative maps).    

« Percentage of team must be killed for a round failed » : This option will affect only cooperative maps. You can define the number of team death before the round restart 
Example : - if you enter 100 % every players must be dead before the round restart 
                -if you enter 50%, once half of the players are dead the round restart 
    
« Monster Power Level » : Let you define the damage you will receive when a monster hit you. 
  
« Marines Ability » : Let you define shooting precision of the marines 
  
« Monster Health Level » : Let you define health points of the monsters (the less they have, the faster they will die) 
  
« Production of Monster maker » : Some cooperative maps have monster makers, you can define how many monsters can be creates by them 
  
« Speed Production of Monster maker » : Here you can configure the speed production of these monster makers 
  
                See the next page : 
  
« Friendly fire » : If this option is activate, your shoots can touch your friends in cooperatives maps, so you have to be caution. 
  
« Punish team Killer » : This option complete the friendly fire. If a player kill too many team mate, he will die and stay in ghost during all his existence. 
  
« Junior New Be » : Decrease the damage you are receiving from any units (pfff ! this option is for cowards :-)) 
  
« Maximum Number Of Rounds X 1,5 » : Increase by 50% the max number of round before the cooperative map is lost. 
  
« Game Master Mode » : Lets the Game Master menu appear (works only with cooperative maps). You can by this menu spawn every monsters of the game where you want (be caution, there isn’t a max number of monsters, so if you spawn too many monsters, Half Life may crash).   

Once you finished with the option click DONE and OK to launch a game.

9- How to map for SBO

       a/ Configuration of your Valve Hammer Editor (new Worldcraft). 

With the MOD you laid out of two reserved files for the mapping. They are the DARK.FGD, which contains all the entities specific to SBO and the ANZIMUT.WAD which contains all our textures. You can arrange the DARK.FGD where you want on your hard disk, but it is important to let the ANZIMUT.WAD in the Syndicate directory. 

 For making pretty maps, it is necessary to configure its editor of level. For that, launch the editor. Go in Tools menu, then in Option. Then configure your window as on the image below by using your site of files well on. 

                                                                        

 To create a Syndicate configuration it is necessary to click on Edit then New. 

 Here the list of Wads to be used : 

                                                                        


OK your editor is now to configure for SBO. 
    
            b/ To create a map in co-operative mode. 

 First of all most important: to indicate to the game that your map is played in cooperative mode, it is necessary to add "co _" in front of the name of your map. 
                               
                            1/ Entities of map configuration. 

 The maxround 
 

This entity defines the number of round which your players will have to finish your map. By defaut your players will have right has 5 rounds, and you cannot go down below 3 rounds the option is called "number of round, min3" 
  
The Limitator 
 This entity makes it possible to configure the quantity of ammo per weapon. It is possible to configure all the weapons available to the players. 
The option is in the form : « ammo for auto shotgun max 900 » 
   
The ammo_reserve 
 This entity represents a box of ammo, it is not necessary to configure it. 
 
The failled mission 
If you called this entities by an event in your map, the total failure of the mission is declared. 
  
The game_end 
 You call this entity to declare the end victorious of your map. 

 

The change_touch

 

This entity runs the victorious end  of the mission if a player touch it. During the part it is materialized by a man.

                            2/ The spawn entities 

The co-operative MOD is limited to 8 players. You can, use a single spawn zone, or several zone of spawn which will start conceal events in your map. 

 
The single spawn zone 
This zone of spawn is to constitute of 8 info_player_deathmatch. They are spaced the ones of the others, the walls and ceilings.(cf. image below) 
   

                                                                    

 

Multi zone spawn

 It is possible to create a succession of spawn zone (2 to 5). 
It is simple, when a zone is called (by a trigger_once for example), the zone actually used is disable. And with the round following the players will spawn in the active zone.  

We change the spawn zone when we trig the entity multispawn.

To create the first zone use 8 info_player_deathmatch, spaced ones of the others, the walls and ceilings. 

 To create the second zone use 8 info_player_go2, spaced ones of the others, the walls and ceilings. 

 To create the third zone use 8 info_player_go3, spaced ones of the others, the walls and ceilings... 
 ... 

                            3/ The particles entities 

the entity :  fummee_particules

 

By generating particles, this entity lets you create some realistic smoke effects

Configuration options are:

Name : in order to give a name to the entity :-).

Nombre de particules : setup the number of particles used at the same time by one entity

Greater is the number, more beautiful is the result. But in order to prevent crash and lag, you shouldn't go up to 50.

 

Setup of smoke size and effect :

-Taille de la fumee  --> smoke size

-Largeur de la base de la fumée --> width from the base of the smoke

-Taille de départ de chaque particule  --> starting size for each particle

-Taille maximum de chaque particule  -->maximum size for each particle

 

Setup of particles speed and move :

By generating particles, this entity lets you create some realistic rain or snow effects

                            4/ The monsters entities  

There are two category of IA in our mod. The traditional version where the monster stays in the zone which were affected to him by the mapper. And the hunter version, where the monster seeks the players in order to kill them. It moves and track the players, it reacts  to the noise, and the most savage can smell you .

 The ideal one is to use the entity monster_macker, which  allow the monster of your choice to spawn regularly. You can, by that way, maintain a constant number of monsters present in the map, by compensating for the monsters died by newcomer.


To populate your map, you have the choice between several monster crueler the ones than the others. 
  
Marines (available in hunter version) 

                                                                                                    

To place marines in your map use the entity monster_human_grunt. You can configure the weapon which they carry : HG being "Hand Grenade" and GL " Grenade Launcher".

These marines also exist in zombie version under the name monster_zombie_grunt, To kill hem you have to shoot them one bullet in the head. 

The marines also exist with heavy weapons (sniper and gatling) they are available with the entity dark_grunt.
