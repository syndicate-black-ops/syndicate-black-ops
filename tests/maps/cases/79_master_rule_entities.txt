# master on the rule point entities (FGD "master"): while the master (a multisource whose one member has not fired)
# is shut, game_counter, game_counter_set, game_player_hurt and game_text ignore every call; once it
# opens they work. Callers are the world here (the player-activator ones are below)
# covers: game_counter.master game_counter_set.master game_player_hurt.master game_text.master multisource
map co_fuzz
spawn multisource targetname lock
spawn trigger_relay targetname key target lock
spawn game_counter targetname c health 1 target c_hit master lock spawnflags 2
spawn game_counter_set targetname cs target c2 frags 1 master lock
spawn game_counter targetname c2 health 1 target c2_hit
spawn game_player_hurt targetname ph target ph_out master lock
spawn game_text targetname gt message master_text master lock spawnflags 1
activate
connect 1 P1
frames 3
mark locked
fire c world
fire cs world
fire ph world
fire gt world
frames 2
expectnot Firing: (c_hit)
expectnot Firing: (c2_hit)
expectnot Firing: (ph_out)
expectnot MSTR 23 ->#1: master_text
mark open
fire key
fire c world
fire cs world
fire ph world
fire gt world
frames 2
expectn 1 Firing: (c_hit)
expectn 1 Firing: (c2_hit)
expectn 1 Firing: (ph_out)
expectn 1 MSTR 23 ->#1: master_text
