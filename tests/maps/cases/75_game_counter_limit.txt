# game_counter (FGD "Fires when it hits limit"): each On / Toggle call counts up from the Initial Value (frags); it
# fires its target when the count equals the Limit Value (health). Without flag 2 "Reset On fire" it then counts on
# past the limit and never fires again; with flag 2 it goes back to the initial value and fires at every limit
# covers: game_counter game_counter.health game_counter.frags game_counter.target game_counter:2
map co_fuzz
spawn game_counter targetname c_plain health 3 target plain_hit
spawn game_counter targetname c_reset health 3 target reset_hit spawnflags 2
spawn game_counter targetname c_init health 3 frags 2 target init_hit
activate
frames 3
fire c_plain
fire c_reset
fire c_init
frames 1
expectn 1 Firing: (init_hit)
fire c_plain
fire c_reset
frames 1
expectnot Firing: (plain_hit)
fire c_plain
fire c_reset
frames 1
expectn 1 Firing: (plain_hit)
expectn 1 Firing: (reset_hit)
mark past_the_limit
fire c_plain
fire c_reset
fire c_plain
fire c_reset
fire c_plain
fire c_reset
fire c_init
fire c_init
frames 1
expectn 1 Firing: (plain_hit)
expectn 2 Firing: (reset_hit)
expectn 1 Firing: (init_hit)
