# infodecal (FGD): without a name it puts its decal (texture) on the world as the map starts (a static decal, sent to
# every client that joins) and removes itself; with a name it waits until triggered, then sprays the decal
# (TE_BSPDECAL, temp entity 13, at its origin) and removes itself
# covers: infodecal infodecal.texture
map co_fuzz
spawn infodecal texture {shot1 origin "100 200 300"
spawn infodecal targetname named texture {blood1 origin "400 500 600"
activate
frames 3
expectre STATICDECAL 100.0 200.0 300.0 decal=1 
expectnotre STATICDECAL 400.0
expectcount infodecal 1
expectnotre MSG svc23 ->\w+: 13 
mark fire
fire named
frames 3
expectre MSG svc23 ->\w+: 13 400 500 600 1 
expectcount infodecal 0
