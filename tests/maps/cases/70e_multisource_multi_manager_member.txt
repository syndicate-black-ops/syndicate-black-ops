# multisource with a multi_manager member (registered because the manager lists the multisource as a target) and a
# trigger_relay member: the manager's call counts as its own slot, so both together open it
# covers: multisource multi_manager
map co_fuzz
spawn multisource targetname ms target ms_out
spawn multi_manager targetname mm ms 0.5
spawn trigger_relay targetname r1 target ms triggerstate 2
activate
frames 3
fire mm
frames 10
expectnot Firing: (ms_out)
fire r1
frames 2
expectn 1 Firing: (ms_out)
mark mm_again
fire mm
frames 10
expectn 1 Firing: (ms_out)
