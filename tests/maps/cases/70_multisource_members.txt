# multisource (FGD "fires when every input is on"): its members are the entities whose target is its name; it fires
# its target when every member's slot is on; a member's second Use turns its slot off again (toggle)
# covers: multisource multisource.target trigger_relay.target trigger_relay.triggerstate=2
map co_fuzz
spawn multisource targetname ms target ms_out
spawn trigger_relay targetname m1 target ms triggerstate 2
spawn trigger_relay targetname m2 target ms triggerstate 2
activate
frames 3
mark m1_on
fire m1
frames 2
expectnot Firing: (ms_out)
mark m2_on
fire m2
frames 2
expectn 1 Firing: (ms_out)
expect Multisource ms enabled (2 inputs)
mark m1_off
fire m1
frames 2
expectn 1 Firing: (ms_out)
mark m1_on_again
fire m1
frames 2
expectn 2 Firing: (ms_out)
