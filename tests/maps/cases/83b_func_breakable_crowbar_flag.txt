# breaks it at once; the stub cannot aim a crowbar (no trace hits), so this case checks the other side of the rule:
# club damage from something else (a trigger_hurt with damage type 128 Club) is not instant, it only counts double
# (HL SDK: breakables take double club damage), with or without the flag. Health 100, 2 x 20 club per touch = 40:
# both still stand after one touch and break at the third
# covers: func_breakable:256 trigger_hurt.damagetype=128

cvar mp_juniorpvmonst 1
cvar mp_juniorpowmonst 1

cvar mp_makermon 1

cvar mp_hardcore 100
cvar mp_friendlyfire 1
cvar mp_flashlight 1

spawn func_breakable model *2 health 100 target no_flag_broke material 1
spawn trigger_hurt model *3 dmg 40 damagetype 128

touchent 2 1

dump 0
