Syndicate Black Ops Alpha 1.                          05/2002

 

Installation: This mod is a Zip format mod, you need winzip, winAce or any other decompression softwares.

 

Once it is decompressed, put the “syndicate” file in your Half life index. The best is to have the latest version of the patch for Half life. To play at this mod you will need a 1107 or higher version.

 

The content of the alpha:

 

-          Some of the models present in this mod aren’t ours, for any information, contact us. For the beta 1, we have replaced the models that didn’t belong to us by our models.

 

-          The teamplay is only half inserted in this version, owing to bugs we could only put the team deathmatch version and the capture zone version, even the previous has little bugs.

 

-          The following game mods work and are in in the alpha: deathmatch, royal battle, cooperative and team deathmatch. Some problems may arise in the round system when deconnecting or reconnecting. If this occurs try to have the last player commit suicide. This bug will be settled later.

 

-          The weapon recoil and events System only could be applied as “chaingun”. The other weapons are with the former recoil system. All the weapons will have the new system in the alpha 2.

 

-          In some game mods, the rounds don’t start immediately, it is not a bug! But a system of “check”.

 

-          We have not definitively chosen the weapons used for this mod, we contemplate to replace the P90 by two ingrams, the deserteagle by a more futurisistic gun of our creation with a bigger calibre. The Sg55 will be replaced too. An heavy plasma thrower and a autocanon will appear for the next versions.

 

-          The cyberthechnology is introduced and works.

 

-          The shooting management through the walls is only managed for the barret, it doesn’t work in the cooperative because of gameplay reasons, the next versions will have other weapons which will have this function.

 

-          The management of the influence of the equipment on the speed is implanted and work, but is not used for the royal battle because of gameplay reasons and fun.

 

-          The management of armour localizations and damages is operational.

 

If you have some ideas, suggestions, bugs to point out, critiques to submit to us, or if you want to take part in the development , we are waiting for you here: http://perso.wanadoo.fr/sbo/

 

I hope you will like this alpha and that you will find pleasure in playing at it as we do. If we do this alpha, it is neither for a glory that we will never have nor to satisfy our ego, but only to allow people to find pleasure in playing at our mod and maybe to motivate them to join the team. See you soon for the alpha 2 and then the beta.
