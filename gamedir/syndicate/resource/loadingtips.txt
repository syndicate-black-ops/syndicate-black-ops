The circle at the left of the chat line opens a color wheel: Chat sets your chat color, Name your name color.
Up and Down in the chat line bring back the lines you sent before.
Find server remembers your filters and where you put its window.
On a dedicated server, Discord friends can join you from your profile while Discord shows what you are playing.
A double click on a server in Find server joins it; Enter does the same.
Find server shows each server's mode, human players and bots, and a picture of its map.
