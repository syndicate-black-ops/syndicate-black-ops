Mapped by Roux.

Level of the map : MEDIUM

The Federation try to put into orbit an observation satelite. It is enough sophisticated to locate the secret buildings of Croon. Delay the 
takeoff of the launcher in order to leave enough time to our engineers to improve the satellite jammers.

Mission :

- Sabotate the launcher.

Your are in operation, don't kill your team mate.
The round starts after a certain time when at least one of players chooses 
his equipment.
The round will be finished when all the players will be died or when the objectives of 
mission will be successful.

Maximum number of rounds before a failed mission : 21 

Experience System :
-You gain xp points each time you wound an ennemy.
-When you have a sufficient number of xp points, you gain a level.
-When you die, you lose xp points. 
-If you kill a fellow-member, you lose 5 levels.

Max players : 8
