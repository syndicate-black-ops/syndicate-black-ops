Level of the map : HARD

Aemos Federal Colony. Croon has installed a research and development base in one 
of their buildings. During the Zeta primus incident, a gate has been opened in the 
colony which results to Outside world forces invasion. Unlike than for the others 
incidents, Federal marines were in the place. They controlled the invasion and 
stood the siege. Now they stand again, and Croon dislikes this situation because 
no one and particularly not the federation should know.
That's why Croon orders us to eliminate any trace of its experiment.


Mission :

- Eliminate the 7 scientists who are surviving.

- Stop the TerraForming factory.
 
- Turn off the 3 thermic evacuation tube in order to make the atmosphere disapearing.
 Federals troop has created near this points some siege zones.
 
- Destroy the Croon building. You can find a remote control for the federal satelite, 
in one of the marine's post.
 
- Take the artefact in the bar.
 
But be carefull Agent. There's a very strong cyborg also in the aera.
 
Glory to the Syndicate !

Your are in operation, don't kill your team mate.
The round starts after a certain time when at least one of players chooses 
his equipment.
The round will be finished when all the players will be died or when the objectives of 
mission will be successful.

Maximum number of rounds before a failed mission : 21 

Experience System :
-You gain xp points each time you wound an ennemy.
-When you have a sufficient number of xp points, you gain a level.
-When you die, you lose xp points. 
-If you kill a fellow-member, you lose 5 levels.

Max players : 8
