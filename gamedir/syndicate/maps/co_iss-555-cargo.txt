Mapped by Roux.

Level of the map : MEDIUM

A vessel cargo liner of our fellow-members Space unlimited has not given anymore news. It transported for us some delicate things
to the eyes of the laws federal. You must go in this vessel, recover the bord information, and do the housework.

Mission:

- Recover the 6 modules of information under the command center

- Recover the module of information of the hypersleep rooms.

- Open the access to the hoppers of maintenance of the engines, the
  switch is in the command center

- Start the system of self-destruction in the cooling chamber of
  the engine 

- Evacuate the space ship


Your are in operation, don't kill your team mate.
The round starts after a certain time when at least one of players chooses 
his equipment.
The round will be finished when all the players will be died or when the objectives of 
mission will be successful.

Maximum number of rounds before a failed mission : 21 

Experience System :
-You gain xp points each time you wound an ennemy.
-When you have a sufficient number of xp points, you gain a level.
-When you die, you lose xp points. 
-If you kill a fellow-member, you lose 5 levels.

Max players : 8
