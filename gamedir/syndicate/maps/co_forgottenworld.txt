Mapped by Anzymut

Level of the map : MEDIUM

There's an unamed and dark secret in the mysterious Gagny's quarries. Discover This secret.

Objectives : Discover the secret.

Your are in operation, don't kill your team mates.
The round starts after a certain time when at least one of players chooses 
his equipment.
The round is finished when all the players died or when the objectives of 
mission are finished.

Maximum number of rounds before a failed mission : unknow !!!

EXPerience System :
-You gain xp points each time you wound an ennemy.
-When you have a sufficient number of xp points, you gain a level.
-When you die, you lose xp points. 
-If you kill a fellow-member, you lose 5 levels.

Max Players : 8
